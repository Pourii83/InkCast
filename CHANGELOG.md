# Changelog

## v0.2.0

- **Crafting jobs**: creating several items at once shows the item count and the
  total time of the whole job; the bar fills against the whole job and a glow
  sweeps across it once per item.
- **Hunters**: the swing bar now also times Auto Shot and wand Shoot (in green);
  with SuperWoW, casts such as Aimed Shot also show on the player bar.
- **Looks**: six bar textures (`/ink texture smooth|gloss|minimal|banto|blizzard|flat`),
  color-tinted bar background, soft shadow, bordered icon.
- Cast bars show `elapsed/total` time, with the total in a smaller font.
- Renamed from OctoCast to **InkCast**; commands are now `/inkcast` and `/ink`.

## v0.1.0

- Player cast bar with latency and pushback, GCD bar, melee swing timer and
  SuperWoW target cast bar for the 1.12 client.
